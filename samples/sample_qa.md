### What is a Consolidated Account Statement and how do I get one?

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is a single combined statement that shows an investor’s monthly transactions across all mutual funds and other securities held in demat accounts. To obtain a CAS, you must update your PAN card number with your depository participant or stock broker.  
Source: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/consolidated_account_statement.html
